## Autopilot for Your Job Search in LATAM

### 🎯 The Problem

**Job searching is broken in LATAM.**

- 6+ hours weekly manually browsing job boards
- 200+ applications for 1 interview (2,000:1 ratio for juniors)
- No visibility into application status
- Outdated CVs = missed opportunities
- Limited access to market intelligence

**The Market:** 15M+ tech workers in LATAM, growing 25% YoY. Remote work revolution created global competition.

**JobBot = Autopilot for Your Tech Career**

| 📊 **Application Tracking** | Pipeline management |
